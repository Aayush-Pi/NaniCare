# NaniCare — 3-Minute Demo Runbook

## 1. Pre-Demo Setup (30 seconds)
1. Start both servers with `./start.sh` from the repository root (`http://localhost:3000` frontend, `http://localhost:8000` backend).
2. Confirm the top-right status pill shows **FastAPI · Neon** (or **FastAPI · SQLite**).
3. In **Caregiver Mode → Verified Schedule**, click **Reset DEMO01** if needed so `DEMO01` starts clean with 5 medicines.

## 2. Caregiver Mode: AI Scan & Safety Gate (60 seconds)
1. Open **Caregiver Mode → Scan & Verify**.
2. Upload a handwritten prescription photo (or click **Run Sample Prescription**).
3. Point out:
   - **Yellow Highlight Gate:** Low-confidence (`< 0.80`) or missing-strength fields are highlighted in amber/yellow so the caregiver must verify before publishing.
   - **Regimen Safety & Interaction Banner:** Drug-drug and timing interactions (e.g., Thyroid + Calcium 4-hour gap, Blood thinner + NSAID bleeding risk) surface automatically.
4. Choose **Flush previous & replace** (or **Add to existing schedule**) and click **Flush & Publish** to save to PostgreSQL/SQLite.

## 3. Grandparents Mode: Zero-Auth Voice Companion (45 seconds)
1. Switch to **Grandparents Mode** (`DEMO01`).
2. Switch language between **English**, **हिन्दी**, and **मराठी**, then click **Read My Medicines Aloud** to play Indian neural voice audio (`POST /tts` via `edge-tts`).
3. Tap **I Took This** on any dose card (including Early Morning `Thyronorm`), then refresh the page to show persistence.

## 4. Vitals & Family WhatsApp Loop (45 seconds)
1. Open **Vitals** and log a reading (e.g. Temp `101.6°F`, BP `148/92`, Weight `58.5 kg`, plus optional Pulse/SpO2/Blood Sugar).
2. Show the deterministic clinical alert flag and the **Daily Family WhatsApp Report** generated by `GET /profile/DEMO01/summary`.
3. Click **Share on WhatsApp (`wa.me`)** to show the pre-filled family health digest link.
